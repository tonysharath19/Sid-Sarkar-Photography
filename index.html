Chasing light, slowly.

Images for people who feel a photograph before they read it. Rooted in India, drawn to the world. From monsoon mornings in Bengaluru to the last light over the Western Ghats.

Bengaluru, India · 12.9716° N

“I don't take photographs - I wait for them. For the breath between two gestures, the fog that lifts for seven seconds, the stranger who forgets I'm there. Everything else is just patience and a very good lens.”

Philosophy"Light is the only subject. Everything else is a place for it to land."

I'm Sid Sarkar, an Indian photographer based in Bengaluru, drawn to quiet landscapes, restless cities, and the people who give them life. My visual language is shaped by India's colours, monsoon light, and everyday rituals.

From intimate portraits to editorial journeys, I work with natural light and a patient eye. I'm based in Karnataka and available for stories across India and around the world.
